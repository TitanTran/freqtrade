Why the Strategy is "Stupid" (Strategic Analysis)

1. The "Safety Net" is Too Low

The stoploss is set at -12% (-0.12). At 5x leverage, this is a 60% loss of your margin. This isn't a stoploss; it's a liquidation buffer. It doesn't protect capital.

2. Missing Profit Protection

trailing_stop is False. Even if a trade goes into 3% profit, the bot won't secure it. It will wait for the slow 1H trend exit, often turning a winner into a loser.

3. Conflicting Timeframes

You are scalping on 15m but exiting on 1H trends. By the time the 1H EMA 20 crosses EMA 50, the 15m price has already moved 2-4% away from your exit point.

4. Low AI Conviction

In Trade #24, the FreqAI score was likely borderline. The strategy allows entries with scores < -0.01, which is very loose for a precision short.
